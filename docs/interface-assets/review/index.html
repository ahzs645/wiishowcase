Complete visual review: 66 screens, 264 captures

Every screen was manually inspected at 640×480, 854×480, 960×720 and 1280×720, with prior and available source overlays. This page records the findings, subsequent corrections and what still differs.

Interactive opacity/difference overlays · Additional screenshot references · Review ledger and image hashes · Targeted rendering checks

A completed review does not mean a completed replica. No screen is certified pixel-identical. 8 screens had no native source in this review’s original evidence set. The supplementary collection adds examples for three of them. Related states, crops, firmware/region differences and low-resolution illustrations remain labeled.

Manually inspected all four current captures per state on contact sheets with 50% source and prior overlays. Full-resolution images and a live browser were used to verify suspected defects. Static review is separate from interaction tests. The preserved inspection sheets show revision 4a5fa10 before this pass. Current images below show the corrections; their individual overlay links open the updated viewer. 20 affected screens received a visual follow-up.

  • No screen is certified pixel-identical. Authentic licensed font files do not establish native glyph size, pane metrics or region/firmware matching.
  • Most sources are low-resolution 4:3 manual illustrations. Centered contain overlays preserve aspect; borders, crops and opposite aspect images are not geometrically registered.
  • Different content, selection states and dates are identified rather than scored as pixel error.
  • Missing or related references cannot establish native accuracy.
  • Phone Quit/OK were checked in the original capture and a live browser and are intact; the initial reduced-overview suspicion was withdrawn.
